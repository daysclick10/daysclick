DayClick is designed to simplify the workflow between content creation and publishing, helping creators manage their social media channels more efficiently.

Content Management

Organize videos, captions and publishing information before distributing content to your connected social media accounts.

Scheduling

Choose when your content should be published and manage upcoming posts through a centralized scheduling workflow.

Multi-platform Publishing

How DayClick works

Connect your accounts, prepare your content, choose your publishing settings and schedule when your posts should go live.

1. Connect

Authorize supported social media accounts securely using the platform's official authorization process.

2. Prepare

Select your video and prepare captions, descriptions and other publishing information.

3. Schedule

Select a publishing date and time for your content.

4. Publish

DayClick sends the authorized publishing request to the selected social media platform.

Built for creators

DayClick aims to reduce repetitive publishing work so creators can spend more time producing content and less time managing uploads.
