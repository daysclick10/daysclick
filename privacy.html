Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

DayClick is an AI-powered short-form video creator focused on producing original visual stories, creative experiments and entertaining digital content.

This Privacy Policy explains how DayClick handles information when users interact with our website, services and supported third-party platform integrations.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features being used, DayClick may process limited information including:

  • Basic account information provided by authorized third-party platforms.
  • Information required to authenticate connected platform accounts.
  • Videos, captions, descriptions or other content submitted to DayClick.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure our services.
  • Messages or information voluntarily provided when contacting DayClick.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and improve DayClick services.
  • Provide requested content-related features.
  • Authenticate authorized third-party platform connections.
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse.
  • Troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Respond to user questions or support requests.

3. AI-Generated Content

DayClick uses artificial intelligence and other creative technologies to assist in the creation of short-form videos, visual concepts and digital content.

AI-generated or AI-assisted content may be edited, reviewed or combined with other creative materials before publication.

4. Third-Party Platforms

DayClick may interact with third-party services such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook through their official systems and APIs.

When a user authorizes a third-party platform connection, DayClick only accesses information permitted by that authorization and required for the requested feature.

Third-party services operate independently and are also governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Authorization Credentials

Where required, authorization credentials or tokens may be processed to maintain an authorized connection with a third-party platform.

Users may revoke third-party access through the relevant platform's account or security settings.

6. Sharing of Information

DayClick does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms or service providers only when necessary to provide an authorized feature, operate the service, maintain security or comply with applicable law.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements or resolve technical issues.

8. Data Security

DayClick uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or misuse.

9. User Choices

Users may stop using DayClick or revoke access to connected third-party platforms at any time through the relevant platform's settings.

10. Children's Privacy

DayClick is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent or other legal authorization is required.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DayClick develops or as legal and platform requirements change.

The latest version will always be available on this page.
