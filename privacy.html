Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

DayClick is a social media content management and scheduling service designed to help creators manage and publish content across supported social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DayClick, we may process information necessary to provide the service, including:

  • Basic account information provided by connected social media platforms.
  • Authorization tokens required to connect supported social media accounts.
  • Content information such as videos, captions, descriptions and publishing settings.
  • Scheduling information such as selected publishing dates and times.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only as necessary to provide and improve DayClick, including:

  • Connecting authorized social media accounts.
  • Preparing and publishing user-requested content.
  • Managing scheduled publishing tasks.
  • Displaying publishing status and account information.
  • Maintaining security and troubleshooting technical issues.

3. Social Media Platform Data

DayClick may connect to third-party platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook through their official authorization and API systems.

DayClick only accesses platform information that a user explicitly authorizes and that is necessary to provide requested features.

4. Authorization Tokens

Where required, DayClick may securely store authorization tokens so that connected features can continue to operate without requiring the user to sign in for every publishing action.

Users may disconnect their accounts or revoke access through the relevant social media platform.

5. Sharing of Information

DayClick does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to connected social media platforms when necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as publishing content.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide DayClick's services, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, or complete user-requested publishing tasks.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect account information and authorization credentials from unauthorized access, disclosure or misuse.

8. User Choices

Users may stop using DayClick, disconnect connected accounts, or revoke third-party authorization at any time through the relevant platform's account settings.

9. Third-Party Services

DayClick relies on third-party social media platforms and their APIs. Use of those services may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of those third-party platforms.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DayClick develops. The latest version will always be published on this page.
